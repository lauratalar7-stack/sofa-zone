Delivery, Returns & Warranty Policy

Delivery Information

We aim to deliver all orders as quickly as possible.

  • Products available in Ireland: Delivery typically takes 2–4 business days.
  • Products not currently in stock in Ireland: These products must be ordered from our supplier in Poland. Delivery may take approximately 2–4 weeks.

Customers will be informed if their order requires additional delivery time.

Sofa Delivery & Assembly Service

We provide a full delivery and assembly service for all sofas.

Our team will:

  • Deliver your sofa directly to your home.
  • Assemble the sofa for you upon delivery.

If a sofa is found to be faulty during assembly or upon delivery, our team will first attempt to repair the issue on the spot. If the fault cannot be repaired, we will replace the sofa with a new one at no additional cost.

Returns Policy

Customers may return products within 30 days of delivery, provided that:

  • The product is unused and in its original condition.
  • The product is free from damage and faults caused by misuse.

For approved returns made within 30 days, customers will receive a 90% refund of the purchase price. Delivery charges are non-refundable unless required by law.

Warranty

All products are covered by a 1-year warranty from the date of delivery.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and faults that occur during normal use. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, improper care, or normal wear and tear.

If you experience an issue covered by the warranty, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
